# European larch bark swatch

OpenAI ImageGen generated the photorealistic bark swatch. The included image was resized to 512 × 512. The build equalizes tile edges and estimates normal, roughness, occlusion, and height maps from the image. Needle and cone textures are procedural.

## Generation prompt

> Square seamless photorealistic base-color material scan of mature European larch bark (Larix decidua), straight-on orthographic macro filling entire frame. Rough layered bark with deep mostly longitudinal irregular narrow crevices, short overlapping jagged ridges, dark gray-brown weathered outer scales and realistic muted cinnamon-russet bark underneath across some cracks. Fine natural fibers, even flat cross-polarized diffuse lighting with no hard shadows or specular reflections, a half metre square swatch, no vegetation, borders, text, objects or trunk silhouette. Photographic realism, no illustrated outlines or repetitive geometric plates.
